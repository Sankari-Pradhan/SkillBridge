❓ FAQs & Help

What is SkillBridge?

SkillBridge helps users explore careers, learn skills, and create resumes.

How can I create a resume?

Open Create Resume from the dashboard and fill in your details.

How can I analyze my resume?

Open Resume Analyzer, upload a supported resume file, and click Analyze.

How can I track my learning?

Open Learning Progress and tick the topics you have completed.
